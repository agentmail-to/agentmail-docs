---
title: "How do I know when an email is opened?"
subtitle: Track opens on the emails your agent sends, and react when a recipient reads one.
slug: knowledge-base/open-tracking
---

Use open tracking. When you send with `track_opens`, AgentMail adds an invisible 1x1 pixel to your HTML body, served from your own domain. The first time a recipient's mail client loads that pixel, the message is marked as opened.

You see the open in three places:

- the `opened` label on the message and its thread, which you can filter on
- the message's `opened_at` time
- the `message.opened` event, pushed to your webhook or WebSocket

Open tracking is included on every plan at no extra cost. Setting it up takes three steps:

1. **Turn it on for a custom domain.** In the [AgentMail Console](https://console.agentmail.to?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=knowledge-base/open-tracking), open **Domains**, choose **View DNS records** from the domain's actions menu, and turn on the **Open tracking** switch. Or set `tracking_enabled` when you create or update the domain through the API.
2. **Publish the `link.<domain>` CNAME** at your DNS provider, set to DNS only (not proxied), and wait for the domain's `tracking_status` to be `VERIFIED`.
3. **Send with `track_opens`** on a message that has an HTML body.

<Note>
Opens are a directional signal. Mail clients that block images miss opens, and Apple Mail Privacy Protection, Gmail, and security scanners load images on their own, often within seconds of delivery. Only the first open of each message is recorded.
</Note>
