AgentMail suspends an account when it cannot confirm who owns it at sign-up, when too many accounts were created from the same email domain, or when its mail bounces or draws spam complaints at a high rate. A suspended account keeps receiving and reading email but cannot send. Only AgentMail support can lift a suspension: email [support@agentmail.cc](mailto:support@agentmail.cc).

## At a glance

| `suspended_reason` | What the API says | What to send support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `unauthorized.signup` | Unverified account ownership at signup | The email address and domain you signed up with, and what your agent is for |
| `signup.domain_limit` | Too many recent sign-ups from your email domain | The email address you signed up with, your company, and whether a teammate already has an AgentMail organization |
| `message.bounced` | A high email bounce rate | Where your agent gets recipient addresses, and what you changed to stop the bounces |
| `message.complained` | A high spam complaint rate | Who your agent emails, why they expect it, and how you handle opt-outs |

## Symptoms

- Sends fail with a `403` whose `code` is `account_suspended`. The body includes `suspended_reason`. On a send, the `message` starts with "Message rejected: Your account has been suspended due to" followed by the phrase in the table above. Other blocked requests, such as creating an inbox, return the `message` "Account suspended".
- The Console shows a suspension banner. For bounce and complaint suspensions, AgentMail also emails the members of your organization, and you may have received a warning email before it.

| Still works | Blocked |
| --- | --- |
| Receiving mail in your existing inboxes | Send, reply, reply-all, forward, and sending drafts, including over SMTP |
| Webhook and WebSocket events for inbound mail | Scheduled sends: when one comes due, its draft's `send_status` becomes `failed` and it is not retried |
| Reading inboxes, threads, messages, and drafts in the API and Console | Creating inboxes |
| `GET /v0/organizations` | Calendar invitation emails |

AgentMail can also suspend a single inbox or pod instead of the whole account. Then only sends from that inbox or pod fail, with the code `inbox_suspended` or `pod_suspended`, and the rest of your account keeps sending.

## Why

`unauthorized.signup` and `signup.domain_limit` are applied at sign-up: when an account is created in the Console, or when a human claims an agent's inbox. `signup.domain_limit` usually means several people at one company signed up separately.

`message.bounced` and `message.complained` come from your sending. AgentMail weighs bounce and complaint signals across your organization's mail and does not publish exact thresholds. Common causes are addresses a model guessed or scraped, old contact lists, and mail recipients did not ask for. AgentMail does not support cold outreach.

## How to check

`GET /v0/organizations` returns `suspended_at` and `suspended_reason` while the account is suspended. It needs an organization-scoped API key. Read the fields as shown below: the API returns them even when your SDK version's `Organization` type does not list them. For bounce and complaint suspensions, the [rates endpoint](https://docs.agentmail.to/api-reference/metrics/query-rates) shows your organization-wide bounce and complaint rates over time.

<CodeBlocks>
```python title="Python"
import os

from agentmail import AgentMail

client = AgentMail(api_key=os.environ["AGENTMAIL_API_KEY"])

# suspended_at and suspended_reason are only present while the account is suspended
org = client.organizations.get()
print(org.organization_id, getattr(org, "suspended_reason", None), getattr(org, "suspended_at", None))

# organization-wide bounce and complaint rates, latest point last
rates = client.metrics.query_rates()
for rate_type, points in rates.items():
    if points:
        print(rate_type, points[-1].rate, points[-1].sent)
```

```typescript title="TypeScript"
import { AgentMailClient } from "agentmail";

const client = new AgentMailClient({ apiKey: process.env.AGENTMAIL_API_KEY });

// suspended_at and suspended_reason are only present while the account is suspended
const org = await client.organizations.get();
const { suspended_reason, suspended_at } = org as { suspended_reason?: string; suspended_at?: string };
console.log(org.organizationId, suspended_reason, suspended_at);

// organization-wide bounce and complaint rates, latest point last
const rates = await client.metrics.queryRates();
for (const [rateType, points] of Object.entries(rates)) {
    const latest = points?.[points.length - 1];
    if (latest) console.log(rateType, latest.rate, latest.sent);
}
```

## What to do

Retrying, creating new API keys, or pausing and resuming inboxes does not lift a suspension. Before you contact support about a bounce or complaint suspension, fix the cause:

- Validate addresses before sending, and never let the model guess or construct an address.
- Handle the `message.bounced` and `message.complained` [events](https://docs.agentmail.to/events), and stop emailing addresses that bounce or complain.
- Honor opt-out replies: when someone asks your agent to stop, add them to your [send block list](https://docs.agentmail.to/lists).
- Email only people who expect to hear from your agent.

For `signup.domain_limit`, if a teammate already has an AgentMail organization, you can ask them to invite you to it instead.

Scheduled drafts that come due while you are suspended fail, so resend or reschedule them once sending is back. After support lifts a suspension, your API key can keep returning `account_suspended` for up to 5 minutes.

## Get help

Email [support@agentmail.cc](mailto:support@agentmail.cc). The support link in the Console banner fills in your organization ID for you. Include:

- Your organization ID, plus the inbox ID or pod ID if the error was `inbox_suspended` or `pod_suspended`
- `suspended_reason` and `suspended_at`, or the full error response and the time of the failed request
- The details for your reason from the table above

If sends fail without `account_suspended`, `inbox_suspended`, or `pod_suspended` in the response, check [status.agentmail.to](https://status.agentmail.to).
