Messages automatically expire after 24 hours when you enable message expiry for
your organization. Keep your agents focused on recent conversations with an
optional, organization-wide access window.

## Request message expiry

[Contact our team](https://www.agentmail.to/enterprise) to discuss availability and
request activation. Message expiry is off by default and is enabled for selected
organizations by the AgentMail team.

## How it works

The 24-hour window starts when AgentMail records the message's creation time.
The sender's email date and later label updates do not restart the window.

Once a message expires:

- It no longer appears in message lists or search results.
- Fetching the message or its content returns not found.
- Threads show only messages that are still accessible. Threads with no accessible messages are no longer available.
- Replies, forwards, and drafts that need to read the expired source message cannot use it.

The setting applies to **existing and new messages**. Once activation takes
effect, existing messages older than 24 hours are also inaccessible.

## Activation and existing copies

Our team will confirm when activation takes effect. Cached access settings may
take several minutes to refresh. Download links issued before activation can
remain usable until their original expiry, normally up to one hour. New message
content links issued under the expiry policy cannot outlive the message's
access window.

Previously downloaded content, delivered webhooks, and content copied into
drafts or other systems are independent copies and are not withdrawn by expiry.

## Access expiry and deletion

Expiry removes customer access; it does not guarantee deletion of all backend
copies within 24 hours. It is an access policy, not a zero data retention or
permanent deletion guarantee. Disabling the setting restores access to retained
messages.
