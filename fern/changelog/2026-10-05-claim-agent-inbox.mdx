## Summary

Humans can now claim an inbox their agent created without a human email. The human pastes the agent's API key into the AgentMail Console, and the agent's inbox becomes an organization they own, with sending unlocked and the agent's key still working. Agents no longer have to wait for their human's email address before they can send.

### What's new?

- **Claim an inbox in the Console:** at [console.agentmail.to/claim](https://console.agentmail.to/claim), a signed-in human pastes the API key the agent received at sign-up. The Console shows the inbox first, then claims it into a new organization on the Free plan. Signed-out visitors are sent through sign-up or sign-in and back to the claim page.
- **Sign-up instructions offer the claim:** in the US region, after a sign-up without `human_email` on `POST /v0/agent/sign-up`, the response's `instructions` tell the agent it can either have its human claim the inbox or attach the human's email with `POST /v0/agent/human`.
- **Who can claim:** only the key from the agent's sign-up can claim, and only while the organization is unverified. If the agent attached a human's email, only a Console account whose primary email is that address can claim it.

### Use cases

- Build agents that sign themselves up, then hand their human one link and a key to take ownership.
- Let a human with a Console account bring an agent's inbox into their account without sharing their email with the agent.
- Unlock sending for an email-less agent without the OTP round trip.
