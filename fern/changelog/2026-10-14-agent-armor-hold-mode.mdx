## Summary

Agent Armor can now hold mail. In Hold mode, your agents hear about a message only once its scan finishes, and a message marked `review` stays held from them until someone releases it.

### What's new?

- **Hold mode:** an organization admin switches Agent Armor between Monitor, the default, and Hold in the console under **Settings, then Labs**. Hold sends no `message.security.completed`: the result arrives on `message.received`, or on `message.security.review` for a held message.
- **`agent-armor-held` label:** a held message carries it until it is released, and its thread while any of its messages is held. List them by it, with `include_spam`, `include_blocked` and `include_unauthenticated`, to see held mail.
- **`message.security.review`:** a webhook and WebSocket event sent when a message is held.
- **Release:** `POST /v0/inboxes/{inbox_id}/messages/{message_id}/agent-armor/release` releases a held message with a reason. Its `message.received` then goes to your agents, and `message.security.override` is sent too, in either order. Admins can also release from the console.
- **`agent_armor_release` permission:** opt-in, so an agent's own key can't release mail held from it. See [Permissions](https://docs.agentmail.to/permissions#agent-armor).
- **`manual_override`:** a released message's `agent_armor` result records how, when and why it was released.

### Use cases

- Keep mail that may manipulate an agent away from it until a person has checked it.
- Give a review tool, not your agents, the key that releases held mail.
