## Summary

Agent Armor is now in AgentMail Labs. An organization admin can turn it on from the console, and every message your inboxes receive is scanned for attempts to manipulate an AI agent.

### What's new?

- **Agent Armor in Labs:** after joining AgentMail Labs, an organization owner or admin turns on Agent Armor under **Settings, then Labs**. It is free while it is in Labs.
- **Verdicts on messages:** a scanned message carries an `agent_armor` result. A `complete` result has a verdict of `clear` or `review`, with a short reason.
- **`message.security.completed`:** a webhook and WebSocket event sent when a scan finishes.
- **Feedback:** `POST /v0/inboxes/{inbox_id}/messages/{message_id}/agent-armor/feedback` records whether a verdict was right.
- **Never in the way:** Agent Armor never holds, delays or changes mail.
- **Availability:** US production (`api.agentmail.to`), not yet EU production (`api.agentmail.eu`).

### Use cases

- Keep instructions hidden in inbound email from reaching your agent's tools unchecked.
- Route suspicious messages to a person before your agent replies.
